Today, I presented our new work (jointly with *Henri Lefebvre*, *Oleg A.
Prokopyev* and *Martin Schmidt*) on **"Bilevel Optimization with a
Piecewise-Linear Follower"** for the first time at the [EUROYoung Workshop
2026](https://euroyoung.eu/lugano2026/) in Lugano. You can already check out all
the numerical results contained in our computational study
[here](https://simstevens.github.io/pwl-bilevel-numerical-study/index.html). The
preprint will follow shortly. Stay tuned!
