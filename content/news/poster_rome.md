I had the pleasure of participating in the poster session at the [MIP Europe
Workshop 2026](https://www.mixedinteger.org/EUROMIP/2026/) in Rome and presenting
our recent work on **"Bilevel Optimization with a Piecewise-Linear Follower"**
(jointly with *Henri Lefebvre*, *Oleg A. Prokopyev* and *Martin Schmidt*). You
can already find all our numerical results
[here](https://simstevens.github.io/pwl-bilevel-numerical-study/index.html).
Stay tuned for the paper!
